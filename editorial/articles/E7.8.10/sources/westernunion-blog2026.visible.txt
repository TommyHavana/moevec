Frequently asked questions
How can I send someone money immediately?
You can send and receive money quickly with Western Union using our website, mobile app, or in person at an agent location. Many transfers may be available for pickup in minutes
5
, depending on service type, destination, and receiver method.
How do I send money to someone with Western Union?
Sending money with Western Union is simple, whether you prefer digital or in-person transfers.
To send money online or in the Western Union
®
app:
Register or log in and verify your profile with a driver’s license, passport, or government-issued ID.
Choose your receiver’s country and how much you want to send.
Select how they’ll receive the money — cash pickup, bank account, or mobile wallet (where available).
Enter your receiver’s details.
Pay for your money transfer service via credit or debit card or with your bank account.
We’ll provide a tracking number (MTCN) to help you and your receiver track the transfer.
You can also complete a Western Union money transfer at a nearby agent location.
Does Western Union have a digital wallet?
The Western Union
®
app is not a digital wallet; it’s a money transfer app allowing you to send money, track transfers, and pay bills. You can use the app to
send money to mobile wallets
in various countries, depending on your receiver’s digital wallet provider.
