抄録
The housing bonus FAR type district planning is the method for securing housing in the inner area. But, does this method work for this area including many small sites?
The result of the case studies proves that the method has the limited effect on securing housing in the area. The reason is that the condition of supplying housings is not good enough for each land owner, and the smallness of each site limits the housing supply in the inner area.
