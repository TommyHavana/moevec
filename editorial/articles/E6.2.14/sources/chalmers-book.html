What if there’s a better way to help people in poverty?

Bestseller When Helping Hurts has changed the way thousands of church and ministry leaders approach poverty. Designed to equip you to begin effectively helping low-income people, When Helping Hurts articulates a biblical framework for poverty alleviation.

Learn how to walk with the materially poor in humble relationships instead of just providing them temporary handouts.
