header1 Overview

Internet freedom in Cuba declined during the coverage period. A severe energy crisis, marked by prolonged nationwide blackouts, caused major disruptions to internet connectivity,1 while affordability challenges deepened.2 Authorities continued to censor and punish online dissent by blocking independent news sites,3 imposing multiyear prison sentences on individuals for their online expression,4 and conducting systematic digital surveillance.5
