Digitally delivered services trade dataset

(Updated July 2026)
The dataset contains WTO estimates on services traded through computer networks, such as the Internet, apps, emails, voice and video calls, and digital intermediation platforms. It comprises over 200 economies and regions covering eight sub-sectors for the period 2005-25. The coverage of digitally delivered services reflects the definition of the IMF-OECD-UNCTAD-WTO Handbook on Measuring Digital Trade.
