(a) Original Proceeding

Date Development
November 22, 2013 The Secretary-General registers a request for the institution of arbitration proceedings.
February 21, 2014 Following appointment by the Claimants, Francisco Orrego Vicuña (Chilean) accepts his appointment as arbitrator.
March 4, 2014 Following appointment by the Respondent, J. Christopher Thomas (Canadian) accepts his appointment as arbitrator.
April 30, 2014 The Claimants file a request for the Chairman of the Administrative Council to appoint the arbitrator not yet appointed pursuant to Article 38 of the ICSID Convention.
August 6, 2014 Following appointment by the Chairman of the Administrative Council, Eduardo Zuleta (Colombian) accepts his appointment as presiding arbitrator.
August 7, 2014 The Tribunal is constituted in accordance with Article 37(2)(b) of the ICSID Convention. Its members are: Eduardo Zuleta (Colombian), President, appointed by the Chairman of the Administrative Council in accordance with Article 38 of the ICSID Convention; Francisco Orrego Vicuña (Chilean), appointed by the Claimants; and J. Christopher Thomas (Canadian), appointed by the Respondent.
September 23, 2014 The Tribunal holds a first session in Paris.
October 6, 2014 The Tribunal issues Procedural Order No. 1 concerning procedural matters.
November 14, 2014 A non-disputing party files an application to file a written submission pursuant to ICSID Arbitration Rule 37(2).
November 28, 2014 The Claimants file a memorial on the merits.
December 5, 2014 Each party files observations on the non-disputing party’s application.
December 15, 2014 The Tribunal issues a decision on the non-disputing party’s application to file a written submission pursuant to ICSID Arbitration Rule 37(2).
January 30, 2015 The Respondent files a request to address the objections to jurisdiction as a preliminary question.
February 16, 2015 The Claimants file observations on the request to address the objections to jurisdiction as a preliminary question.
March 2, 2015 The Tribunal issues a decision on the Respondent’s request to address the objections to jurisdiction as a preliminary question; as a result, the objections to jurisdiction are joined to the merits of the dispute.
July 31, 2015 The Respondent files a memorial on jurisdiction and a counter-memorial on the merits.
September 29, 2015 Following exchanges between the parties, the parties file a request for the Tribunal to decide on production of documents.
October 14, 2015 The Tribunal issues Procedural Order No. 2 concerning production of documents.
November 6, 2015 The Tribunal issues Procedural Order No. 3 concerning production of documents.
December 9, 2015 A non-disputing party files an application pursuant to ICSID Arbitration Rule 37(2).
December 18, 2015 The Claimants file a reply on the merits and a counter-memorial on jurisdiction.
December 21, 2015 Each party files observations on the non-disputing party’s application.
December 23, 2015 The Respondent files a request for the Tribunal to decide on production and confidentiality of documents.
January 7, 2016 The Claimants file observations on the Respondent’s’ request for the Tribunal to decide on production and confidentiality of documents.
January 28, 2016 The Tribunal issues Procedural Order No. 4 concerning production and confidentiality of documents.
February 2, 2016 The Claimants file a request for the Tribunal to decide on confidentiality of documents.
February 5, 2016 The Tribunal issues Procedural Order No. 5 concerning the confidentiality of documents.

The Tribunal issues a decision on the non-disputing party’s application to file a written submission pursuant to ICSID Arbitration Rule 37(2).
February 18, 2016 A non-disputing party files a request for reconsideration of the Tribunal’s decision of February 5, 2016.
February 22, 2016 Each party files observations on the non-disputing party’s request.
February 26, 2016 The Tribunal issues a decision on the non-disputing party’s request for reconsideration of the Tribunal’s decision of February 5, 2016.
March 1, 2016 The Respondent files a rejoinder on the merits and reply on jurisdiction.
April 15, 2016 The Claimants file a rejoinder on jurisdiction.
August 8, 2016 The Tribunal issues Procedural Order No. 6 concerning submission of new evidence.
September 12, 2016 The President holds a pre-hearing organizational meeting with the parties by telephone conference.
September 15, 2016 The Tribunal issues Procedural Order No. 7 concerning the organization of the hearing.
October 19, 2016 - October 25, 2016 The Tribunal holds a hearing on jurisdiction and the merits in Paris.
November 2, 2016 The Tribunal issues Procedural Order No. 8 concerning procedural matters.
December 13, 2016 The Tribunal issues Procedural Order No. 9 concerning the admissibility of a new document.
December 16, 2016 Each party files a post-hearing brief.
January 20, 2017 The Claimants file a statement of costs.
January 31, 2017 The Respondent files a statement of costs.
July 21, 2017 The parties submit a joint request for the Tribunal to decide on the admissibility of new documents.

The Tribunal decides on the admissibility of new documents.
July 26, 2017 Each party files observations on the new documents.
November 23, 2017

The Respondent files a request for the Tribunal to decide on the admissibility of new evidence.
November 28, 2017

The Claimants file observations on the Respondent’s request of November 23, 2017.
November 29, 2017

The Tribunal decides on the admissibility of new evidence.
February 26, 2018

The Tribunal declares the proceeding closed in accordance with ICSID Arbitration Rule 38(1).
March 7, 2018

The Respondent files a request to reopen the proceeding pursuant to ICSID Arbitration Rule 38(2).
March 12, 2018

The Claimants file observations on the Respondent’s request of March 7, 2018.
April 16, 2018

The Tribunal issues Procedural Order No. 10 concerning the Respondent’s request of March 7, 2018.
June 15, 2018

The Tribunal renders its award.

(b) Rectification Proceeding

Date Development
July 25, 2018

The Secretary-General registers a request for rectification of the award filed by the Kingdom of Spain.
September 3, 2018

Antin Infrastructure Services Luxembourg S.à.r.l. and Antin Energia Termosolar B.V.  file observations on the Respondent’s request of July 24, 2018.
September 14, 2018

The Kingdom of Spain files a reply on Antin Infrastructure Services Luxembourg S.à.r.l. and Antin Energia Termosolar B.V. observations of September 3, 2018.
September 24, 2018

Antin Infrastructure Services Luxembourg S.à.r.l. and Antin Energia Termosolar B.V. file a rejoinder on the Respondent’s reply of September 14, 2018.
October 2, 2018

Following the passing away of arbitrator Francisco Orrego Vicuña (Chilean), the Secretary-General notifies the parties of the vacancy on the Tribunal and the proceeding is suspended pursuant to ICSID Arbitration Rule 10(2).
November 18, 2018

Following the passing away of arbitrator Francisco Orrego Vicuña (Chilean), Klaus Reichert (Irish/German) appointed by the Claimants, accepts his appointment as arbitrator in accordance with ICSID Rule 11(1).
November 19, 2018

The Tribunal is reconstituted. Its members are Eduardo Zuleta (Colombian), President, appointed by the Chairman of the Administrative Council in accordance with Article 38 of the ICSID Convention; Klaus Reichert (Irish/German), appointed by the Claimants; and J. Christopher Thomas (Canadian) appointed by the Respondent. The proceeding is resumed pursuant to ICSID Arbitration Rule 12.
January 29, 2019

The Tribunal issues a decision on the rectification of the award.

Date Development
May 23, 2019

The Secretary-General registers an application for annulment of the award filed by the Kingdom of Spain and notifies the parties of the provisional stay of enforcement of the award.
August 19, 2019

The ad hoc Committee is constituted in accordance with Article 52(3) of the ICSID Convention.  Its members are: Cavinder Bull (Singaporean), President; José Antonio Moreno Rodríguez (Paraguayan); and Nayla Comair-Obeid (French/Lebanese); all members appointed by the Chairman of the Administrative Council.
September 3, 2019

The Kingdom of Spain files a request to continue the stay of enforcement of the award.

Antin Infrastructure Services Luxembourg S.à r.l. and Antin Energia Termosolar B.V. file observations on the Kingdom of Spain’s request to continue the stay of enforcement of the award.
September 12, 2019

The Kingdom of Spain files a reply in support of its request to continue the stay of enforcement of the award.
September 16, 2019

Antin Infrastructure Services Luxembourg S.à r.l. and Antin Energia Termosolar B.V. file a rejoinder on the Kingdom of Spain’s request to continue the stay of enforcement of the award.
September 18, 2019

The ad hoc Committee holds a first session by telephone conference.
September 20, 2019

The ad hoc Committee issues Procedural Order No. 1 concerning procedural matters.
September 26, 2019

A non-disputing party files an application pursuant to ICSID Arbitration Rule 37(2).

Each party files observations on the non-disputing party’s application.
October 21, 2019

The ad hoc Committee issues a decision on the stay of enforcement of the award.
October 22, 2019

Each party files further observations on the non-disputing party’s application.
November 13, 2019

The ad hoc Committee issues a decision concerning the non-disputing party’s application of September 26, 2019.
November 21, 2019

The Kingdom of Spain files a request for the admissibility of new evidence.
November 29, 2019

Infrastructure Services Luxembourg S.à r.l. and Energia Termosolar B.V. file observations on the Kingdom of Spain’s request for the admissibility of new evidence.
December 13, 2019

The Kingdom of Spain files a response to Infrastructure Services Luxembourg S.à r.l. and Energia Termosolar B.V. observations of November 29, 2019.
December 18, 2019

The Kingdom of Spain files a memorial on annulment.

December 20, 2019

Infrastructure Services Luxembourg S.à r.l. and Energia Termosolar B.V. file a reply on the Kingdom of Spain’s request for the admissibility of new evidence.

The ad hoc Committee issues Procedural Order No. 2 concerning the Kingdom of Spain’s request for the admissibility of new evidence.

The ad hoc Committee issues Procedural Order No. 3 concerning the Kingdom of Spain’s request for the admissibility of new evidence.

Infrastructure Services Luxembourg S.à r.l. and Energia Termosolar B.V. file a counter-memorial on annulment.

The Kingdom of Spain files a reply on annulment.

A non-disputing party files an application pursuant to ICSID Arbitration Rule 37(2).

Each party files observations on the non-disputing party’s application.

Infrastructure Services Luxembourg S.à r.l. and Energia Termosolar B.V. file a rejoinder on annulment.

The ad hoc Committee issues a decision concerning the non-disputing party’s application of August 3, 2020.
