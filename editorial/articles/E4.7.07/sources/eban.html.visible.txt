EBAN – European Business Angel Network 
– is Europe’s largest association of business angel networks and other early stage investors. Our mission is to drive successful and responsible early stage investing by providing our members network connections, best practices and representation towards governmental stakeholders. Our association is a network of networks that gathers thousands of professional investors across Europe and the rest of the world.
