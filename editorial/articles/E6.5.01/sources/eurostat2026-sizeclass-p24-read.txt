Size of businesses
Enterprise size class structure of key indicators in the business economy
(%, share of all size classes, EU, 2023)
Note: enterprises, estimates made for the purpose of this 
publication. Some shares do not sum to 100.0% for reasons 
of rounding.
Source: Eurostat (dataset code: sbs_sc_ovw)
In 2023, the overwhelming majority (99.1%) 
of active EU businesses were micro or small 
enterprises employing fewer than 50 persons. 
Their economic weight was lower in terms of both 
employment and value added: micro and small 
enterprises employed just under half (48.3%) 
of the EU’s business economy workforce while 
contributing just over one third (34.9%) of the value 
added.
There were 54 200 large enterprises (with 250 or 
more persons employed) in the EU’s business 
economy. These large enterprises represented just 
0.2% of the total number of enterprises. However, 
their economic weight was considerably larger: 
large enterprises employed more than one third 
(36.5%) of the EU’s business economy workforce 
and generated an even larger share of its wealth 
(49.1% of value added).
