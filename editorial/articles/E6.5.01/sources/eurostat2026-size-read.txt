Enterprise size class structure of extra-EU trade in goods
(%, share of all size classes, EU, 2023)
Note: excluding enterprises with unknown size, which 
account for 0.1% of exports and 0.3% of imports.
Source: Eurostat (dataset code: ext_tec01)
In 2023, large enterprises (250 or more employees) 
carried out the majority of extra-EU trade in goods, 
accounting for 71% of the EU’s exports and 61% of 
its imports. Micro enterprises (fewer than 
10 employees) traded the smallest shares of 
extra-EU exports and imports. While large 
enterprises had a larger share of exports than 
imports, the opposite was observed for the other 
size classes. 
For example, micro enterprises’ share of imports 
was 1.7 times as large as their share of exports.
