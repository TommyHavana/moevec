The Economy 2.0: Microeconomics
 
 
 The Economy 2.0: Microeconomics is a free, introductory textbook to microeconomics and the economy. It can be used for undergraduate level courses and in a wide range of settings, including graduate non-economics programmes, self-study, and high-school courses. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The Economy 2.0: Macroeconomics
 
 
 The Economy 2.0: Macroeconomics is a free, introductory textbook to macroeconomics and the economy. It can be used for undergraduate level courses and in a wide range of settings, including graduate non-economics programmes, self-study, and high-school courses. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Understanding our Economy
 
 
 Understanding our Economy is a free textbook for beginner and first-year economics students, students at two-year and four-year colleges and universities in North America, and high school students with an interest in economics. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Experiencing Economics
 
 
 A free ebook of experiments and games for learning economics by ‘experiencing it’ that you can run with your students online or in-the-classroom. Step-by-step instructions and accompanying freely available software make experiments easily accessible. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Doing Economics
 
 
 If you want to learn data-handling, software and statistical skills by working through policy problems using real data, try out CORE Econ’s Doing Economics. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
CORE Insights
 
 
 CORE Insights are a self-contained educational resource with a variety of CORE Econ’s standard student learning activities. The aim of the Insights series is to provide additional, topical, teaching and learning resources linked to coverage in CORE Econ’s ebooks. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The Economy 1.0
 
 
 The Economy 1.0 is CORE Econ’s original free, open access text for a year long introductory undergraduate course. It includes capstone units devoted to globalization, inequality, the environment, innovation and the networked economy, and the role of government. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Economy, Society, and Public Policy
 
 
 If you want to know how economics can help in understanding important policy problems like sustainability, inequality and the future of work, try out CORE Econ’s Economy, Society, and Public Policy. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The Economy: A South Asian Perspective
 
 
 The Economy: A South Asian Perspective (TESA) is an adaptation of The Economy introduction to economics textbook. It uses the style, framing and content of the original introduction to economics ebook and introduces topics and features specific to South Asian and developing economies. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
La economía 2.0: Microeconomía
 
 
 La economía 2.0: Microeconomía es un libro de texto gratuito de introducción a la microeconomía y a la economía. Puede utilizarse en múltiples entornos y niveles educativos, tales como estudios de grado, de posgrado para no economistas, de enseñanza secundaria o de autodidactas. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
L’Économie
 
 
 L’Économie est un manuel de CORE Econ, en accès libre et destiné aux étudiants d'un cours d'introduction à l'économie. Reposant sur des données empiriques et des exemples concrets tirés du monde réel, le manuel repose sur un programme actualisé proposant le meilleur de la recherche économique moderne. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
L’Économie pour le Lycée
 
 
 Three interactive, open access texts to introduce French high school students to economics. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
L’economia
 
 
 L’economia è il libro gratuito e ad accesso libero di CORE Econ, pensato per corsi introduttivi di lauree triennali. Attraverso un’attenzione specifica su dati empirici ed esempi dalla realtà di tutti i giorni, L’economia aggiorna il curriculum di studio, incorporando il meglio della ricerca economica moderna. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
La Economía
 
 
 La Economía es el libro de texto gratuito y de libre acceso de CORE para cursos introductorios a la disciplina económica en pregrado. Con énfasis en datos empíricos y ejemplos de la vida real, este actualiza al programa para incorporar lo mejor de la investigación moderna en economía. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Talous
 
 
 Talous on CORE Econ:n maksuton, kaikille avoin teos taloustieteen kandidaattitason johdantokursseille. Se keskittyy empiirisiin aineistoihin ja tosimaailman esimerkkeihin ja tuo opetukseen modernin taloustieteen uusinta tutkimusta. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
A Economia
 
 
 Uma completa introdução à ciência econômica e à economia, usada com sucesso em cursos de graduação e em diversos outros ambientes, incluindo programas de pós-graduação em áreas não-econômicas, estudo independente e cursos de ensino médio. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Die Wirtschaft
 
 
 Die Wirtschaft ist das kostenlose, frei zugängliche Lehrbuch von CORE Econ für Grundlagenveranstaltungen. Es motiviert Studierende durch die Verwendung von Daten und realen Problemen und aktualisiert den Lehrplan, um das Beste aus der modernen Volkswirtschaftslehre einzubeziehen. 
 
 
 Find out more
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Ekonomika, spoločnosť a verejná politika
 
 
 Ak chcete zistiť, ako vám ekonómia môže pomôcť pochopiť dôležité problémy politík ako udržateľnosť, nerovnosti a budúcnosť práce, siahnite po knihe Ekonomika, spoločnosť a verejná politika od tímu COR EconE. 
 
 
 Find out more
