Spain’s IPRI score increased by 0.743 to 7.144 placing it 15th in the Western Europe region and 22nd in the world. Spain is classified by the IMF as part of the Advanced economies group and by the World Bank as High income country.

 

Spain’s Legal and Political Subindex decreased by -0.002 to 6.205 with scores of 6.147 in Judicial Independence, 6.646 in Rule of Law, 5.768 in Political Stability, and 6.259 in Control of Corruption.

 

Spain’s Physical Property Rights Subindex increased by 2.241 to 8.283 with scores of 10 in perception of Property Rights Protection, 6.248 in Registering Property, and 8.6 in Ease of Access to Loans.

 

Spain’s Intellectual Property Rights Subindex decreased by -0.013 to 6.943 with scores of 6.299 in perception of Intellectual Property Protection, 7.217 in Patent Protection, 7.686 in Copyright Protection, and 6.571 in Trademark Protection.
