Chile’s IPRI score increased by 0.104 to 6.03 placing it 3rd in the Latin America & Caribbean region and 39th in the world. Chile is classified by the IMF as part of the Latin America and the Caribbean group and by the World Bank as High income country.

 

Chile’s Legal and Political Subindex increased by 0.016 to 6.44 with scores of 7.094 in Judicial Independence, 6.25 in Rule of Law, 5.479 in Political Stability, and 6.937 in Control of Corruption.

 

Chile’s Physical Property Rights Subindex increased by 0.323 to 6.233 with scores of 10 in perception of Property Rights Protection, 5.198 in Registering Property, and 3.5 in Ease of Access to Loans.

 

Chile’s Intellectual Property Rights Subindex decreased by -0.03 to 5.416 with scores of 6.162 in perception of Intellectual Property Protection, 7.167 in Patent Protection, 3.043 in Copyright Protection, and 5.291 in Trademark Protection.
