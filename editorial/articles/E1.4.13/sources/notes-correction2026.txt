This corrects the article "Community notes reduce engagement with and diffusion of false information online" in volume 122, e2503413122.
Correction for “Community notes reduce engagement with and diffusion of false information online,” by Isaac Slaughter, Axel Peytavin, Johan Ugander, and Martin Saveski, which published September 18, 2025; 10.1073/pnas.2503413122 (Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A.
122, e2503413122).
The authors note that the following competing interest should be added to this paper:
“J.U. has collaborated with individuals employed by Meta within the past 48 months. M.S. has collaborated with individuals employed by X/Twitter within the past 48 months. The authors’ collaborators were not aware of the results presented in this work until they were publicly available. The authors declare no competing financial interests.”
Clarifying language was added to the PNAS competing interest policy after the authors’ original submission.
The article has been updated.
