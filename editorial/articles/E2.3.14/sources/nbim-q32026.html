    Key figures Q3 2026

    The fund returned -0.5 percent in the third quarter of 2026, or -278 billion kroner, in accounting terms. This was 0.18 percentage point lower than the return on the benchmark index.

    The fund's value

    22,014
    billion kroner


    At the end of the third quarter of 2026, the fund had a value of 22,014 billion kroner. The market value of the investment portfolio was 22,025 billion kroner.

    The equity investments had a market value of 15,875 billion kroner, fixed-income investments 5,649 billion kroner, unlisted real estate investments 363 billion kroner and renewable energy infrastructure 114 billion kroner. Equities made up 72.1 percent of the fund, fixed income 25.8 percent, unlisted real estate 1.6 percent and renewable energy infrastructure 0.5 percent.

    Billion kroner
    • See the fund's value since 1998
    The fund’s return was close to flat, ending slightly negative as rising interest rates led to weaker results on our fixed-income investments. Equity returns were also close to zero, but there were large differences between sectors. Energy companies rose the most, while interest-rate-sensitive sectors such as utilities and real estate fell,” says Deputy CEO Trond Grande of Norges Bank Investment Management.
    Trond Grande
    Deputy CEO of Norges Bank Investment Management
