General Information:

The Eritrean diaspora’s contributions towards strengthening Eritrea’s political, economic, and social development dates back to the days of the armed struggle for independence.  Inspired by this history and in an effort to make this noble practice systematic and sustainable, the Government in 1995 issued the Proclamation on the Recovery and Rehabilitation Tax.

In accordance with the provisions of the Proclamation, eligible Eritreans who live abroad contribute 2% of their net income to rebuilding Eritrea.  Payment of the tax gives them political and economic rights on par with those who reside in the country and have fulfilled their obligations.  These include the right to obtain land for business or residential purposes.

Recovery and Rehabilitation Tax Requirements:

Self-employed

Both of the following documents must be presented:

  • IRS Individual Wage and Income Transcript
  • Social Security Earnings Record

Unemployed

Required Documentation

The following two documents must be presented:

  • Social Security Earnings Record
  • IRS Individual Wage and Income Transcript

The following additional documents must be presented, if applicable:

  • School Transcript – if enrolled in school during the relevant period
  • Disability or Benefit Statements – if disability or other benefits were received during the relevant period

Salaried Employee

Both of the following documents must be presented as proof of income:

  • Social Security Earnings Record
  • IRS Individual Wage and Income Transcript

Both documents are mandatory. Submission of only one document will not satisfy the proof-of-income requirement.

Retiree

Retirees must present documents listed below (Only once):

  • Social Security Statement – Earning Record
  • Form 1099-R

FAQ – Recovery and Rehabilitation Tax 

What if I am a full-time student?

You may not be required to make a payment if you do not have earned income.  However you are still responsible for providing the necessary supporting documents and obtaining your RRT clearance every year.
