About the Value Proposition Canvas

Design the offer customers would choose

Why use the Value Proposition Canvas?

Precisely define your customer profiles

Describe one customer segment: the jobs they are trying to get done, the pains they hit along the way, and the gains they are after.

Visualize the value you create

List what you offer, then name the pains it relieves and the gains it creates.

Achieve product-market fit

Fit is a claim until customers confirm it. Test the matches you drew against evidence, then adjust.
