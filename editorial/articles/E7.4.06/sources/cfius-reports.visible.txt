Consistent with Section 721(m) of the Defense Production Act of 1950, as amended, CFIUS transmits an annual report to appropriate members of Congress and makes an unclassified version of the report available to the public, as appropriate and consistent with safeguarding national security and privacy. Public versions of CFIUS’s annual reports and related tables may be found below.
