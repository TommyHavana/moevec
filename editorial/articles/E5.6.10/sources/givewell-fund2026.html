Donate to high-impact, cost-effective programs—backed by evidence and analysis

Donations to the Top Charities Fund are used to support highly cost-effective funding opportunities at global health and development programs whose cost-effectiveness and impact we have a high degree of confidence in. These funding decisions are informed by our latest research, which we conduct for every grant we make.

In addition to meeting our general criteria for grantmaking, the programs supported by the Top Charities Fund also meet more specific criteria, including:

  • We’ve directed a significant amount of money to this program and seen it operate effectively.
  • We think there’s a high likelihood of substantial direct impact from funding this program.1
  • The program has funding gaps that meet our current cost-effectiveness bar.

Donate

How we make grant decisions

We engage in ongoing conversation with our Top Charities about their plans and funding needs, review funding opportunities, and then make grants to high-priority opportunities our research identifies. Our grantmaking decisions take into consideration factors such as:

  • Which funding gaps we expect to be filled and unfilled
  • Each organization’s plans for additional funding
  • The cost-effectiveness of each funding gap

For more detail on past decisions, see the table below.

What you can expect if you donate to the Top Charities Fund

GiveWell grants 100% of the value of donations designated for the Top Charities Fund (minus any fees charged by payment processors) to the program or programs that the research team recommends.

GiveWell does not take any fees from the donations.

We send a follow-up email to all Top Charities Fund donors after their donations are committed and provide estimates of the impact those donors had with their donation.

The impact of Top Charities Fund grants

We estimate the impact of each grant we make from our Top Charities Fund. For example, in March 2024 we granted $9 million from the Top Charities Fund to Malaria Consortium. We estimate our funding will reach about 1.5 million children with seasonal malaria chemoprevention (at around $6 per child treated with a full course of preventive malaria medication) and will save about 2,500 lives at the cost of $3,600 per life (source). If that seems surprisingly expensive to save a life, you can learn more about the cost to save a life here.

View the impact of our grants from 2020 onward in this spreadsheet, which includes grants from our Top Charities Fund and most other grants we’ve made or recommended to our Top Charities.

For more information on how we share impact, see How We Produce Impact Estimates.
