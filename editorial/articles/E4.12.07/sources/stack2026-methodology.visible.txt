Survey fielding
The current local response extract includes 39,266 total rows recorded from June 23, 2026 through August 5, 2026, of which 30,903 are marked as valid responses.
The survey was promoted through stackoverflow.com, stackoverflow.blog and our email list to community members that are opted into learning about research opportunities or announcements.
The survey instrument should be referenced from the public Stack Exchange survey repository:
StackExchange/Survey
.
The public call for feedback that informed the 2026 Developer Survey should be referenced from Meta Stack Overflow:
Help shape the 2026 Developer Survey
.
Valid responses
The current valid-response rule is implemented in
mark_valid_responses()
in
R/aggregation.R
. At a high level, the function adds an integer
valid_response
indicator to the response data.
When bot exclusion is enabled and the input data does not already include
is_bot
, the function first runs
flag_bots()
. The current bot flag uses Qualtrics response-quality fields, including reCAPTCHA score, duplicate respondent status, straightlining questions, unanswered questions, progress, duration, and whether
Country
was answered.
Responses are marked valid through either the primary inclusion rule or the substantive-response recovery rule.
Primary inclusion rule:
Consent
is present and equals
"I agree"
.
Employment
is present.
The response is not flagged as a bot when bot exclusion is enabled.
Substantive-response recovery rule:
The response answered all ten core fielding questions listed below.
This recovery rule is evaluated independently of the primary non-bot condition, so a response with
is_bot == TRUE
can still receive
valid_response == 1
when it answered all ten core fielding questions.
Core fielding questions:
Country
Currency
Compensation
TechDropDown
Languages
CodeTest
Databases
CloudPlat
WebFramework
DevIDE
The survey-section scripts use
valid_response == 1
as the inclusion rule for exports, so the substantive-response recovery rule is preserved downstream.
Write-in handling
Role write-ins from
Role_38_TEXT
are reviewed only among
valid_response == 1
responses, normalized to group repeated answers, and compared against existing and analyst-approved Role categories. Reviewed matches can recode
"Other, please specify"
into a final Role category while retaining
Role_Original
and
Role_NLP_Status
; unresolved write-ins remain grouped as
"Other"
in summaries.
SOChangeAddl written responses are eligible for public use only when
valid_response == 1
,
SOChangeAddlConsent
gives permission to share publicly, and
SOChangeAddl
is non-empty. Responses are decoded, translated to English when needed, and manually curated with
SOChangeAddlPublishText
,
SOChangeAddlInclude
, and
SOChangeAddlExcludeReason
; automated text flags are used only to support review, not to make final publish decisions.
Compensation handling
Compensation is analyzed among
valid_response == 1
responses with non-missing
Country
,
Currency
, and positive numeric
Compensation
; values are converted to yearly USD using exchange rates, with a documented Ukraine-specific correction when likely USD values were entered under
UAH
. Compensation statistics and charts use rows where
CompensationStatsInclude
and
CompensationChartInclude
are
TRUE
, excluding rows with multiple quality flags such as very low or very high converted values, country outliers, suspicious raw-number patterns, non-employed respondents, or student/retired roles.
