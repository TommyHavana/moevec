Monthly Economic Report - September 2026

2026-10-14 - By EVE Online Economic Council
Link copied

Data nerds rejoice for the MER for September 2026 is upon us!

Economic Trends

  • Mining Value declined again in September.

  • Mining increased slightly in High Sec and Wormhole space, while Production rose in Low Sec.

  • Destruction showed signs of increasing in Low Sec and Wormhole space.

  • Gas Volume Mined in Wormhole space rose compared with August.

  • Gas volume mined in Vale of the Silent increased by approximately 113% in September compared with August.

  • Low Sec saw a small, brief rise in Ice Volume Mined toward the end of September.

  • Active ISK Delta shifted from +82.2 trillion ISK in August to −144.7 trillion ISK in September.

  • The Mineral Price Index continued to fall.

  • The Module Price Index fell by approximately 4% compared with August.
