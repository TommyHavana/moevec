Health insurance for people resident in Switzerland
Here, people resident in Switzerland can find useful information on the requirement to obtain insurance, premium comparisons, premium subsidies, co-payment, special types of insurance, optional daily allowance insurance, or the health insurance card.
If you would like to calculate or compare compulsory health insurance premiums, you can use the federal authorities’ neutral premium calculator available at
www.priminfo.ch
.
Requirement to obtain insurance
Anyone settling in Switzerland must take out health insurance within three months after taking up residence. After a birth, parents must obtain insurance for the child within the same period.
More about «Requirement to obtain insurance»
Premiums and co-payment
Insurers set the premiums paid by policyholders.
Services are subject to co-payment by policyholders.
More about «Premiums and co-payment»
Special types of insurance
The insured person can opt to increase the deductible freely. With a higher optional deductible the insured person obtains a premium discount accordingly. In agreement with the insurance company the insured person can limit the right of choice of service provider which are then chosen by the insurance company in view of a more cost-effective care. This results in a premium reduction. By choosing a bonus model the insured person benefits from a sinking premium in case the person does not claim services during a certain period of time.
More about «Special types of insurance»
Premium subsidies
The cantons reduce the insurance premiums of policyholders living in modest economic circumstances. Premium reductions are jointly financed by the federal government and the cantons. The cantons are subject to minimum requirements regarding the financing of premium reductions. In return, the federal government provides them with an annual contribution.
More about «Premium subsidies»
Optional daily allowance insurance
Optional daily allowance insurance covers the risk of temporary loss of earnings due to partial or complete inability to work as a result of an accident, illness or maternity.
More about «Optional daily allowance insurance»
Supplementary insurance
Supplementary insurance is governed by the Insurance Policy Act (VVG), together with the general terms and conditions of insurance. Such insurance is optional, and insurers are not obliged to offer cover.
More about «Supplementary insurance»
Health insurance: Health insurance card
Insurers issue their insureds with an insurance card. It helps improve the quality of medical care and simplify the settlement of claims, and can also serve as ID for the electronic patient record.
More about «Health insurance: Health insurance card»

About us
As part of the Federal Department of Home Affairs, the Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH) is responsible for public health in Switzerland; it develops Switzerland’s health policy and works to ensure that the country has an efficient and affordable healthcare system in the long term.
