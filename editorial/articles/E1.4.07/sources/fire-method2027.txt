HOW WE RANK COLLEGES ON 
FREE SPEECH
We Track Controversies  
and How Schools Respond
We document real-world campus controversies involving speech and score how 
schools respond. Schools that protect speech during a controversy can earn up 
to two bonus points. Those that restrict it can lose up to five points. Penalties and 
bonuses decay by one point per year.
We Add It Up
Each school receives an overall score based on student responses, written 
policies, and how the school handled recent speech-related controversies.
