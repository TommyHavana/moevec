​​​​​​​​​​​​
ITU estimates that approximately 6 billion people – or 74 per cent of the world’s population – are using the Internet in 2025. This represents an increase from 60 per cent in 2020, with 1.3 billion people estimated to have come online during that period. However, this leaves 2.2 billion people still offline.
