The Anxious Generation

Jon Haidt’s landmark book on the Great Rewiring of Childhood

By Jonathan Haidt

Since 2024, The Anxious Generation has brought millions of people into the movement that’s dramatically changing how we approach technology in childhood and adolescence. For many, it put words and research to what we were already feeling, and it gave us actions we could take to fix things. The problems — and solutions — outlined in the book continue to be the foundation of our work at the TAG Movement.

About the book

Jon Haidt’s #1 New York Times bestseller documents how the rise of smartphones and social media, as well as the decline of independence and free play, rapidly rewired childhood and caused a global mental health crisis for kids and teens. In the book, Haidt proposes a set of tangible reforms — or norms — that parents, teachers, schools, and governments can take to protect kids, reclaim childhood in the real world, and create a future that allows them to flourish.

The book proposes four new norms for kids and teens in the digital age.
