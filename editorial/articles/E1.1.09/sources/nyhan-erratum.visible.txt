Correction to:
Nature
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06297-w
Published online 27 July 2023
In the version of this article initially published, the variable described in Supplementary Table 36 as measuring Facebook “strikes” for violations of content policies against “Coordinated Inauthentic Behavior” (CIB) is inaccurate and does not reflect enforcement of
the actual CIB policy
, which is not a content-level policy. We therefore removed this row from Supplementary Table 36.
