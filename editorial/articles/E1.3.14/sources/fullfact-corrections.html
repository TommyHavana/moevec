Corrections Log

This is a record of corrections we’ve made in the past 3 years.

  • 23 Oct 2026 How much of the fall in pensioner poverty is ‘thanks’ to the Conservatives?
    We’ve updated this article to reflect that Ms Whately’s comments were made last week, not “earlier this week” as originally stated.
  • 17 Sep 2026 9/11 video doesn’t prove plane didn’t hit South Tower
    An earlier version of this fact check incorrectly suggested that the plane was not visible due to the angle of the footage. As explained above, the video shared on social media was edited to remove the plane from the original footage.
  • 14 Sep 2026 Solar panels do not currently occupy ‘thousands of square miles’ of farmland
    We’ve corrected an error in the final paragraph of this piece which quoted Mr Anderson as saying “thousands of acres”. He actually said “thousands of square miles”.
  • 24 Aug 2026 How much is being spent on benefits?
    The CSJ told us that its analysis of what a family could receive in benefits did not assume that at least one child had a disability or a health condition. Its analysis did not make a comparison with the average salary. We’ve corrected the article to make these points clear.
  • 7 Aug 2026 How many prison places did the Conservatives create?
    An earlier version of this fact check referred to a "13,000 reduction in prison places through closures and other reasons" between 2010 and 2024. The actual unrounded figure was 13,660—we have removed the reference to avoid confusion.
  • 23 Jul 2026 Has the government ended the ‘immunity’ on ‘low-value’ shoplifting?
    We’ve corrected this page to reflect that the relevant provision of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 has not yet come into force.
  • 23 Jul 2026 Has the government introduced a new offence of assault on shopworkers?
    We’ve corrected this page to reflect that the relevant provision of the Crime and Policing Act 2026 has not yet come into force.
  • 15 May 2026 Welfare payments have not exceeded income tax ‘for the first time ever’
    Corrected a misspelling of Helen Whately's name.
  • 29 Apr 2026 Welsh Labour makes inaccurate claim about Reform UK’s prescriptions stance
    We've corrected an inaccurate broadcast date in the first paragraph of this fact check.
  • 20 Feb 2026 No evidence 75% of England’s mayors are Muslim
    This article has been updated to correct the spelling of the name of the London Borough of Newham Mayor Rokhsana Fiaz.
  • 3 Feb 2026 Picture of child with cancer edited to include Nigel Farage
    An earlier version of this fact check correctly identified the boy in the picture as Branson Blevins, but subsequently referred to him incorrectly using a different name - we apologise for the error.
  • 13 Jan 2026 Is the government on course to create three new National Forests in England?
    This piece was corrected on 13 January 2026 to note the National Forest spans 200 square miles of the Midlands, not 200 miles.
  • 8 Aug 2025 ‘Net Zero’ policy costs do not account for 25% of an electricity bill
    An earlier version of this article failed to consider some of the costs which may be associated with Net Zero, and focused only on environmental and social policy costs. We’ve now corrected this, updating our analysis with new figures from Ofgem and others.
  • 16 Jul 2025 Not all immigration removals count as ‘deportations’
    This article was amended on 16 July 2025 to include figures for the number of enforced returns up to 18 May 2025.
  • 17 Apr 2025 Annual spending on state pensions is only set to increase by £31 billion by the end of this parliament
    This fact check gave an incorrect figure for the increase in state pension spending between 2024/25 and 2025/26. The correct figure is £7.8 billion.
  • 11 Apr 2025 Angela Rayner did not gift portrait of herself to Vietnamese politician
    This article has been updated to correct a spelling error.
  • 20 Feb 2025 Viral list of Labour front bench’s work history omits some ‘non-political’ roles
    We have corrected the conclusion of this article to reflect that Jonathan Reynolds worked only as a trainee solicitor, and did not qualify as a solicitor before becoming an MP.
  • 7 Feb 2025 Are this year’s local elections being ‘cancelled’?
    This article was corrected on 7 February 2025 to clarify that May elections will be going ahead as planned in eight unitary authorities. We also updated the article to include more detail about Isles of Scilly and mayoral elections.
  • 31 Jan 2025 Minister wrong to claim IMF forecast UK’s economy will be the fastest growing in Europe
    We’ve updated this piece to remove an inaccurate description of the G7 as a trading bloc.
  • 2 Dec 2024 How many farms will be impacted by the new inheritance tax rules?
    Typo corrected
  • 29 Oct 2024 Is Labour right to claim there’s a £22 billion ‘black hole’ in the public finances?
    This article incorrectly stated that £13.9 billion of the additional pressures identified were offset by reserve funds and other allowances. The correct figure is £13.4 billion.
  • 17 Oct 2024 Video shows coral farm not HAARP research facility around Florida
    This article has been updated to correct the name of the British Geological Society to the British Geological Survey.
  • 17 Oct 2024 HAARP research facility was not behind recent UK Northern Lights sightings
    This article has been updated to correct the name of the British Geological Society to the British Geological Survey.
  • 17 Oct 2024 No, the Northern Lights aren’t caused by HAARP
    This article has been updated to correct the name of the British Geological Society to the British Geological Survey.
  • 13 Sep 2024 'Unelected' Prime Ministers: common or not?
    We've updated this article to clarify that ten of the 28 time the role of Prime Minister changed hands followed an election where they led the party which gained the most seats rather than votes.
  • 14 Aug 2024 NHS performance: how do the four nations compare?
    We made it clear that a chart showed outpatient cases specifically, and that Northern Ireland has not published comparable waiting list data since September 2023.
  • 25 Jul 2024 Social media image showing ‘Muslims paying tribute to Keir Starmer’ is not real
    This article has been updated to include the response from an X account that shared the image.
  • 19 Jul 2024 NHS performance: how do the four nations compare?
    A section on comparisons in cancer care was removed in response to information about upcoming publications from the ONS.
  • 28 Jun 2024 Crime statistics explained - is it rising or falling?
    Corrected full name of CSEW
  • 25 Jun 2024 What is a parliamentary ‘supermajority’?
    This article was corrected on 25 June to clarify that in the US it is a conviction in an impeachment trial, rather than impeachment itself, which requires a two-thirds majority.
  • 13 Jun 2024 Is the tax burden at a 70 year high?
    This article originally stated that the ‘tax burden’ in 2023/24 was 36%. We’ve corrected this to reflect the accurate figure of 36.1%.
  • 10 Jun 2024 Conservative political broadcast claims on tax and mortgages need context
    We corrected a mistake in this article, which previously said that the government had reduced the basic rate of income tax. In fact the government announced a plan to do this in 2022, but later reversed the decision.
  • 10 Jun 2024 Is Labour right to say families are £5,883 worse off under Rishi Sunak?
    This article previously referred to the OBR as the Office for Budgetary Responsibility and has been corrected.
  • 31 May 2024 Spring Budget 2024: fact checked
    Clarified the level of earnings associated with the greatest tax savings.
  • 20 May 2024 Defence secretary repeats claim about £75 billion increase in defence spending
    Corrected date of X post in story
  • 9 May 2024 HS2 has not cost £100 billion to date
    This fact check was corrected to clarify that the eastern leg of HS2 linking Leeds and the East Midlands, not Leeds to Manchester, was cancelled in 2021.
  • 9 May 2024 Emissions camera in Edinburgh is not linked to fines or points
    A previous version of this article said EDAR devices could not detect acceleration, speed or number plates, which was not correct. We changed the rating from partly false to missing context.
  • 7 May 2024 Were 99.75% of people able to cast their vote when voter ID was first introduced?
    We’ve corrected two typos where we previously said the true proportion of voters who were “unable” to cast their vote may be less than 99.75%. We should have said—as is now above—that the true proportion of voters who were “able” to cast their vote may be less than 99.75%.
  • 15 Mar 2024 Photo of busy train carriage is from Cairo, not Sweden
    This article has been updated to amend a typo in the headline.
  • 15 Feb 2024 Has the government failed in its pledge to grow the economy?
    We’ve corrected this article to make clear that the definition of a recession is widely taken to be two consecutive quarters of negative growth, not GDP growth decreasing in consecutive quarters.
  • 6 Feb 2024 Video does not show electric bus on fire in London
    We updated this article to correct a typo in the claim box.
  • 26 Jan 2024 MailOnline corrects ‘fiscal headroom’ headline
    We’ve corrected this article to make clear that the misleading headline appeared in and was amended by MailOnline, not the Daily Mail.
  • 28 Nov 2023 Widespread confusion over number of ‘people’ on NHS waiting lists
    We corrected a mistake in the Welsh waiting list data and added inpatient and day case data for Scotland.
  • 15 Nov 2023 No evidence clip of Sadiq Khan supposedly calling for ‘Remembrance weekend’ to be postponed is genuine
    The article was updated to correct a typo.
  • 9 Nov 2023 Fluoride levels in toothpaste aren’t toxic when used as directed
    We corrected a description of the claim to clarify it was ‘misleading’ to suggest that the fluoride found in toothpaste is "toxic" and not "safe". We also updated this piece to include more information on the recommended daily amounts of fluoride.
